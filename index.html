The principle

Security stands above compliance

Not because compliance does not matter — it is a whole product here — but because a compliance control can be waived, and an attacker does not read your waivers.

Every framework has an exception process. A control gets accepted as a risk, signed off, and the report goes green. Nothing about the estate changed. Do that a few times across a few frameworks and you have built something worse than a gap: a documented, audited, board-reported sense of safety that does not correspond to anything real.

So the order is deliberate. The security finding is the fact. Compliance is an interpretation laid over that fact, and a waiver changes the interpretation only. In SeQontrol a waived finding is still a finding — it stays visible, it stays scored, and its exception carries a mandatory expiry that invalidates itself when the underlying problem changes shape. You can accept a risk. You cannot make it disappear from the screen.

That is what the three words under the logo are ordered by: secure first, because it is the thing that is actually true; compliant second, because it is provable once the first is real; confident last, because confidence earned in that order is the only kind worth having.

What this means in the product

  • Waived drift is still drift. Governance overlays never rewrite the observed facts.
  • Exceptions expire. Mandatory expiry, and automatic invalidation when the finding changes.
  • Never a fabricated pass. A control that cannot be assessed reports "not assessed", never green.
  • Detection is separate from remediation. Detection is read-only; writing is a distinct, consented decision.
  • Findings are not metered. Discovering more never costs more, so nothing rewards looking away.

The platform

The layer underneath is the product

Anyone can ship a scanner. The durable asset is what every scanner sits on: one tenancy model, one connector, one findings substrate, one audit trail, one entitlement catalog.

Tenancy and provider hierarchy

A provider organisation manages a fleet of client tenants, with delegated onboarding, audited impersonation for support, and an opt-in approval gate before a provider acts inside a managed tenant.

One Entra app

The admin consents once, and the grant covers every product's Graph permissions — so enabling a new product never asks for new permissions. Exchange admin, Power Platform, Azure and DNS each need their own one-time setup, and write-back is a separate opt-in.

Shared findings

Every product emits findings into one store with severity, waivers and control-reference tags — which is why a security finding can become compliance evidence with no second integration.

Tamper-evident audit

A hash-chained audit trail across the platform, so you can show a client or an auditor exactly what was checked, what was changed, and who approved it.

Entitlements and billing

A feature catalog gated per plan and wired to metered billing. Packaging and upsell are a platform capability, so expanding a customer is a configuration change, not a project.

Scheduling and reporting

Scheduled scans with live progress, a report engine that produces evidence packs, notifications, feature flags and experiments — shared by every product rather than rebuilt per product.

Straight answers

Where this actually is

Security software is bought on trust, and trust does not survive a discovered exaggeration. So here is the unflattering version.

"Connect once" means the permission grant, not zero-touch

One Entra app covers every product, so your admin consents once and enabling a product never asks for new permissions. It is not zero-touch: each product's connector still has to be switched on by an administrator, write-back is a separate opt-in, and Exchange admin, Power Platform, Azure and DNS each need their own one-time setup. The platform page lists every step rather than letting you find them during onboarding.

Some planes detect but do not yet fix

Where a plane can be remediated app-only, it is. Where it cannot — Exchange, SharePoint site roles, Power Platform and delegated-admin relationships today — the product says so, with the precise reason, rather than guessing or quietly failing.

We are Microsoft-first

Microsoft 365 and Entra are the deep estate. Box and Slack sharing planes ship today, and CompliancePortal covers Google Cloud and AWS through read-only connectors. Everything else is roadmap, and we will not pretend otherwise on a sales call.

Readiness is not an audit opinion

CompliancePortal produces continuous evidence and readiness for the technical controls on the platforms we support. Your auditor still signs the opinion, and the controls that live in people and process are still yours to run.
