The "so what?" layer

PosturePortal is the read-only board that aggregates findings, risk and coverage from every product into one view — per tenant and across the whole fleet. Built for the person who needs the answer, not the raw scan.

In one line: one board for everything SeQontrol sees, across every product and every tenant.
Tell us to get in touch when it lands Licensing

What it does

  • Aggregates findings across every product into one posture view. Because the products share a findings substrate, this is a read across existing data — not another set of scans.
  • Surfaces the top risks and posture scores, with the trend over time rather than a single snapshot.
  • Shows connector health and credential rotation status, so you know which tenants need attention before a scan silently goes stale.
  • Knows what is covered — which products are actually covering what for a given tenant, so a gap on the board is visible rather than assumed.
  • Saved views and annotations — curate the board for an executive summary or a client review, and keep the commentary attached to the data.
  • Read-only by design — it consumes and presents. That is what keeps it fast, safe and cheap to run across an entire fleet.

Why it matters

Sharing, security, email authentication and compliance signals live in different screens. Executives, account managers and virtual CISOs do not want four dashboards — they want one number per tenant, the top risks, the direction of travel, and something they can put in front of a client or a board.

Without an aggregation layer, most of the value of running several scanners is lost to tab-switching. PosturePortal is what makes a multi-product suite feel like one product.

It is a consumption layer, and it is licensed that way

PosturePortal's value is entirely a function of the products feeding it — with one product connected there is not much to aggregate. So it will not be sold as a standalone line item: it comes with SecurityPortal, and with any ShareCare tier. It exists to make the suite worth more than its parts, not to be another invoice line.

Objections, answered straight

"We could build this in a BI tool."

You could — and then maintain the pipeline forever. PosturePortal is native to the findings model and updates continuously with no pipeline to own.

"Is this a product or just a dashboard?"

It is the layer that makes several products feel like one, and it is the screen the budget-holder actually looks at. That is why it is bundled rather than billed.

"Why would I pay for a read-only view?"

You do not. It comes with the products that feed it.

"We only run one product."

Then it will look thin, and we will say so. It earns its place from the second product onwards.
